Privacy Policy — Gunsmith Bench

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Gunsmith Bench does not ask for your name, email, or any account. The game itself works offline. The only data leaving your device comes from the advertising and leaderboard services described below.

Advertising

The free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, AdMob may access your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and basic usage data such as which ads were shown. We do not receive your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location. Google's handling of this data is described in its Privacy & Terms.

Buying Remove Ads stops all ad requests permanently.

Leaderboard

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score is submitted to Apple's leaderboard under your Game Center nickname. This is optional — the game plays normally without signing in.

Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Stored on your device

Your progress, best score, unlocked firearms, language and haptics setting are saved locally and removed when you delete the app.

Age rating and younger users

The App Store age rating for this app is 13+ in most regions (12+ in South Korea, 16+ in Vietnam). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Because the free version serves advertising, the app is not part of Apple's Kids Category and does not participate in any child-directed advertising programme. If you would prefer no ads at all, the one-time Remove Ads purchase stops all ad requests permanently.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer through the app's support page.
